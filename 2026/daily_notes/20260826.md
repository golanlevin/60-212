## Thoughts from the Welcome Questionnaire

#### What concerns, if any, do you have about this course?

1. **Workload**
	* I'm kinda nervous about the **time commitment**, since my p5.js projects from digital media were not as time efficient as i had hoped
	* Not at the moment, if there are any anxiety it would be mostly **workload** related because I have other heavy courses this semester as well. 
	* keeping up with the **work load**.
	* **workload** because i am taking 2 ( frown ) technical cs courses this sem
	* **workload** on to of other classes, but also trying to make things well and not just doing the bare minimum or generic art.
	* I am anxious about the larger **workload** compared to last year. I also worry about not feeling my raw programming skills or computer concept knowledge improve as the class promotes the use of AI assistance with making some parts of our project. I am also concerned that my months away from programming will make the start of the course harder.
2. **Creative and Conceptual Development**
	* I'm anxious about **my creativity not being expressed** in its fullest form. Most of my coding experience has been in practical areas, where the aesthetic and creative aspects haven't been fully explored. I'd say I probably have less experience in knowing how to execute code in a way that creates interesting and unconventional interactions and visuals.
	* I'm concerned I **won't have ideas** that will spark the workflow to begin and be creatively blocked up.
	* Just not being able to **find what I particularly like** or want to get better at. I am open to try new things but I wish I found something that I wanted to stick with.

#### What questions, if any, do you have about this course?

* I'd like to know how interdisciplinary this course can get. I know it's mainly focused on coding and working with p5.js, but to what extent can we **incorporate other technologies** (digital animation, Unity, Blender, web development, etc.)?
* I'd like to know **what these skills can be added up to and be used in**. 
	* ➔ *Please see this [list of some creative-technology studios](../../resources/studios.md)*

#### Is there anything else you'd like me to know?

* I actually hold a rather conservative and skeptical view on AI. Even though I agree that AI could hugely boost productivity, I also feel as if encouraging AI use in classes has restrained me from actually learning the content. This can be seen in a lot of the CS classes I've taken, in which instructors have given up on trying to regulate AI, and everything is just done by AI.
