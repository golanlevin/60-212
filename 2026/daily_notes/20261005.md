### In-Class Exercise

#### Generative Landscape or Still-Life

*(1 hour; worth 15% towards Assignment 5.)*

* An account at RunComfy.com has been activated and configured for you, using [these instructions](https://github.com/golanlevin/p5-comfy-workshop#comfyui-setup). It has set up a cloud server for ComfyUI which you can access remotely during class.
* **Fork** the [beetles](https://openprocessing.org/@golan/3026936) or [statue](https://editor.p5js.org/golan/sketches/uj4fEFSYo) example.
* **Copy** the URL of the live RunComfy.com server provided to you via Discord in class. Note that your sketch will only work while this server is active. **Paste** this URL into the `RunComfyURL` variable in your sketch. 
* In a 512x512 pixel canvas, **create** a p5.js program that generates an extremely extremely extremely SIMPLE abstract "landscape," governed by some randomness. **Populate** your landscape with features that are suitable for your concept: trees, buildings, vehicles, animals, people, trash, seaweed, volcanoes, craters, zombies, etc. You don't have much time, so keep your design *simple*!
* **Make** your sketch generate a simple caption for your landscape, as well. Your sketch should generate a new landscape and a new caption each time the user clicks. If possible, have properties of your landscape be reflected in the caption.
* **Consider**: foreground, middle-ground, and background layers; natural and human-made features; utopia, dystopia; heaven, hell; exterior vs. interior scenes.
* **Save** your project to this OpenProcessing channel.
* **Press** `s` to save a screenshot. **Post** your screenshot and text prompt to the `#55-p5-comfy` channel.
