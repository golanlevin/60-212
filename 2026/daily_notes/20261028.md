### Exercise: Studio Moniker's *Beach*

![thebeach.jpg](img/1028/thebeach.jpg)

**Exercise:**<br/>This exercise is from the *Conditional Design Manifesto* (2012) by Studio Moniker, which proposes using logic as a "method for accentuating the ungraspable."

* Arrange participants in groups of 3-5 persons per table. Distribute colored pens and a large sheet of paper to each group.
* Each turn, find the most empty space on the paper and place a dot in the middle of it.
