## Puppet Show and/or Software Demonstration


### 1. Puppet Show / Demonstration

This work is intrinsically performative and/or interactive. We'll start by watching all the puppets perform. Then we'll move on to other/alternate software responses. For each project:  

1. *Wordlessly*, in about 10-20 seconds: **show** us what your project does before explaining anything.
2. Then, in 30-60 seconds, perform/demo your project while explaining it. Include: 
  - What controls what?
  - What's the most important mapping?
  - What are we supposed to notice?
3. Then, if the vibe is right, allow us to see it a third time. 


### 2. Mapping Critique

After each presentation, we'll complete a [brief Google form](https://forms.gle/S3amhL5fARJM44yk8) that focuses on *mappings as authored relationships*: **What relationship have you invented between your body and the thing you made?**

1. What is the most interesting transformation between the user/performer's actions and the project's response?
2. What additional mapping or behavior could most increase the project's expressive range?

Some dimensions to consider:

* **literal / transformed** (hand→hand versus hand→mouth)
* **continuous / discrete**
* **amplified / attenuated**
* **coupled / independent**
* **one-to-one / one-to-many**
* **obedient / resistant**
