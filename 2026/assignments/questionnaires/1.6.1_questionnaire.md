## Part A. Questions from the Fall 2026 CMU Student Survey on AI

*The four questions in Part A of this questionnaire are from CMU's **Fall 2026 Student Survey on AI**, which has been [developed by CMU's Eberly Center for Teaching Excellence](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/technology/aitools/genai-studentsurvey/index.html).*

*The Eberly Center's questions are being widely distributed to students at the beginning of the fall semester, in order to "help us better understand CMU students' perspectives on generative AI (genAI). Here, 'genAI' refers to artificial intelligence technology that creates new content in the form of text, images, video, audio, or code. Examples of genAI include ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude, etc."*
